## About This Project

This site presents a data-driven exploration of **San Francisco’s 311 complaints**, with a deep focus on **Street and Sidewalk Cleaning** — the city’s most frequently reported issue. The project analyzes complaint trends over time, maps out geographic hotspots, and uses clustering analysis to uncover structural service gaps.

Built using the **Martini Glass narrative structure**, this story moves from broad 311 patterns to a focused investigation of one critical urban issue, combining visualizations, interactive maps, and statistical insights.

### Key Questions Explored:
- What types of complaints are most common?
- When do people report cleanliness issues most often?
- Which neighborhoods face the highest burden?
- How quickly does the city respond to these reports?
- Can we group districts by complaint and response patterns?

This project aims to support more responsive, targeted public services by making complex civic data understandable to a broad audience.
